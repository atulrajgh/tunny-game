game is played with 24 cards A, K, Q, J, 10, 9
J > 9 > A > 10 > K > Q
4 players
Admin overseas the game does not participate
Players join a gallery and are admitted to the playing area by the admin
opposite players form a team
Admin deals 1 random cards to each player to decide who is the starting dealer by the highest card
first hand is dealt with 4 cards
the player to bid first is the next to the dealer in the clockwise direction
the bidding process starts at 50 ends at 140 (multiples of 10)
a player can pass on the bid
once all 4 players pass in a bidding round the highest bidder select a trump that is not disclosed
on the display for a player only their suite is displayed
for the remaining three players only the name
the remaining cards are distributed 2 each to every player
3 players have ask trump button highest bidder has play trump button
trump suite supersedes any other suite card in a play after it is disclosed
if a player has the suite card that is currently in progress they have to play the suite card
The admin and the gallery can watch the game as it unravels
the admin decides the team that wins the round of 6 hands and allocates points to the team that wins the round of 6 tricks
Scoring: declarer team scores 1 pt if HCP >= bid (+1 bonus for 280), defenders score 1 pt if declarer fails (+1 bonus for all 280)
